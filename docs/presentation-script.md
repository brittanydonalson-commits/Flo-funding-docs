# We Elevate — Full Presentation Script

*A complete talking points guide you can follow for presentations, meetings, or conversations.*

---

## THE OPENER — Hook Them

> "What if there was a place in Cameron where women who've given everything to their families — and lost it all — could rebuild their lives? That's what We're building. It's called We Elevate."

---

## WHO WE SERVE

> "We serve women who've been married for years, then suddenly find themselves divorced or widowed — with no income, no job experience, no safety net.
> 
> These women aren't in danger from abuse. They're in danger from something harder to see: financial devastation after years of being a wife and mother.
> 
> They're not 'homeless' in the way shelters typically serve. They need something different. They need a chance to learn skills, build confidence, and become independent — while their kids are cared for."

**[If asked for numbers:]**
> "41% of first marriages end in divorce. Women see a 73% drop in income after divorce — compared to 41% for men. There are 13 million widows in the US, many who never worked outside the home. This gap is real, and it's not being served."

---

## WHAT WE OFFER — The Core

### Housing
> "Safe, temporary housing in small cabins — privacy without isolation. There's a shared community building where everyone eats dinner together each night."

### Farm & Garden
> "We have a working farm and garden. It's not just for food — it's therapeutic. There's something grounding about working with your hands in the earth. Residents work the garden, grow vegetables, and learn to farm."

### Skills & Training
> "We offer workshops: job training, financial literacy, life skills — the real things women need to support themselves."

### Homeschool Co-op
> "Moms lead the homeschool. This gives them purpose and teaching experience while their kids learn. It's not top-down — the residents run it."

### Emotional & Spiritual Support
> "This is a big one. We provide counseling, peer support, grief processing — a space to heal emotionally and spiritually. Some of this comes from partners in the community, some from volunteers, some from faith-based care."

### The Path Forward
> "The goal isn't permanent dependence. The goal is independence. Women leave here with skills, confidence, and a plan."

---

## COMMUNITY INTEGRATION — This Is New

> "But We Elevate isn't just for our residents. It's for the whole Cameron community."

### Community Garden
> "The garden continues as a community garden. Our residents eat from it, but so does anyone in the community who needs fresh produce."

### Community Dinners
> "We host community dinners — starting monthly, then biweekly, eventually weekly. Neighbors gather, share a meal, build real connection. These are open to everyone."

### Seasonal Events
> "We also host seasonal events: a pumpkin patch, watermelon festival, eventually Christmas trees. These are family-friendly community gatherings that also help fund the mission. It's sustainability through community."

### Mental Health & Wellness Resources
> "We want to be a resource for the whole community. Mental health support, counseling referrals, support groups — not just for residents, but for anyone in Cameron who needs it. We're building partnerships with local counselors, churches, and health resources."

---

## WHY CAMERON?

> "Cameron is population 5,306 — small enough to be USDA Rural Development eligible, big enough to have a real community. It's 71 miles from Austin, with a strong church community and affordable land. I believe in this town. I believe there's hardworking people here who want to help each other."

---

## THE VISION — Phased

> "Phase 1: Land secured, first cabins, first residents, community garden and first dinner.
> 
> Phase 2: Expand housing, more programs, community dinners twice a month, first seasonal event (pumpkin patch).
> 
> Phase 3: Full facility, weekly community dinners, year-round events, fully operational."

---

## WHAT WE NEED — The Ask

### Land
> "We need 10-20 acres in Cameron or Milam County. We're looking for a church or organization to partner — either land donation or long-term lease. Land donations to nonprofits are tax-deductible."

### Partners
> "We need partners: churches, mental health professionals, local businesses, nonprofits. If you know someone who might want to be part of this, connect us."

### Volunteers
> "We need people willing to give time — skills, mentorship, hands-on help."

### Funding
> "We need money to build. Startup costs for a 6-unit minimum are roughly $250K-$360K. We don't have personal funds — it's all grants, donations, and fundraising."

---

## CLOSER

> "This isn't just a shelter. It's a community. A place where women rebuild their lives, and the whole community benefits.
> 
> These women gave everything to their families. They deserve a chance to rebuild."

---

## IF THEY ASK...

**"How is this different from a shelter?"**
> "Traditional shelters focus on immediate safety from domestic violence. That's critical — and we don't want to take spots from those in danger. But there's a whole other group of women who aren't in physical danger but are in financial and emotional crisis — and there's almost nothing for them. We fill that gap."

**"How long do women stay?"**
> "We're flexible. It's not one-size-fits-all. Some may stay a few months, some longer. The goal is readiness to live independently — when they have skills, confidence, and a plan."

**"What about the kids?"**
> "Kids stay with their moms. That's by design. We have family cabins, and the homeschool co-op. Keeping families together is part of the healing."

**"How do you fund this long-term?"**
> "A mix: grants, donations, community events, and eventually our own revenue from seasonal events and farm products. The community dinners and events are open to the public — they're both connection and sustainability."

**"Will there be men there?"**
> "We believe in privacy without isolation. There may be men around for certain things — maintenance, farm work. It's not a black-and-white situation. We're focused on the women and children, but we're not building a bubble."
