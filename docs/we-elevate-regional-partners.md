# PART 4: PRE-TRIP PREPARATION

## 📞 Before You Go — Call Ahead

1. **Milam Family Resource Center** — Ask about director, schedule a meeting
2. **First Baptist Cameron** — Ask who handles community outreach
3. **Bell Baptist Association** — Ask about missions/compassion ministries

## 👀 Things to Observe

- Condition of buildings/property
- Community demographics
- Other nonprofits in the area
- Land available for lease/purchase

## 🎤 Questions to Ask

1. "What community needs do you see here?"
2. "Do you know of any women in transition who need support?"
3. "Are there properties or land that might be available for a good cause?"
4. "Would your organization be interested in partnering on a housing ministry?"
