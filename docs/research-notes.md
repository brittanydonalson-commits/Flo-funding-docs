# We Elevate — Research Notes

## Housing Laws (Texas)

### Key Regulatory Areas
1. **Zoning** — Rural/AG zoning in Cameron may limit group housing or require special permits
2. **Building codes** — Cabins, common areas need standard permits
3. **Health & safety** — Well/septic, fire extinguishers, smoke detectors
4. **Tenant rights** — Residents have certain legal protections even in program housing

### Children & Licensing
- **Mothers with their own children** → Providing housing (not a childcare facility) = lighter regulation
- **Caring for children not in your legal custody** → Triggers DFPS licensing (group home, foster care, etc.)

**Recommendation:** Since target population is women with their own children, we should be in the clearer regulatory zone. Confirm with land use attorney once property is identified.
