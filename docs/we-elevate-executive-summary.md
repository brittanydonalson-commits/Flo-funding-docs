# We Elevate
## Executive Summary — One Page

---

### The Vision

A transitional living center in Cameron, Texas that helps women who have no independent income after divorce or widowhood — and their children — rebuild their lives with dignity.

**What we provide:** Safe housing, job training, financial literacy, life skills, a working farm for healing and purpose, and a path to self-sufficiency.

---

### The Problem

| National | Texas |
|----------|-------|
| ~40% of marriages end in divorce | 6,339 families with children homeless |
| Women face 73% income drop after divorce | +39% increase in family homelessness |
| 13 million widows in the US | 80,000+ divorces/year |

**The gap:** Traditional shelters focus on domestic violence. Women who were housewives/mothers with no income — but NOT escaping violence — have nowhere to go. They need a bridge to independence, not just a place to stay.

---

### The Solution

**10 families** at a time live on our cabin-style campus:

- **Duplex cabins** (5 structures) — Each has 2 private units; can house 2 moms with up to 3 kids each OR 1 mom with 3+ kids
- **Common building** — Kitchen, dining, community space
- **Farm & gardens** — Therapeutic work, skill-building, purpose
- **Shared facilities** — Bathroom & shower blocks, laundry

**6-24 month program** with case management, job training, financial literacy, and support.
