# We Elevate
## Executive Summary — One Page

---

### The Vision

A transitional living center in Cameron, Texas that helps women who have no independent income after divorce or widowhood — and their children — rebuild their lives with dignity.

**What we provide:** Safe housing, job training, financial literacy, life skills, and a path to self-sufficiency.

---

### The Problem

| National | Texas |
|----------|-------|
| 41% of marriages end in divorce | 6,339 families with children homeless |
| 73% income drop for women after divorce | +39% increase in family homelessness |
| 13 million widows in the US | 80,000+ divorces/year |

**The gap:** Traditional shelters focus on domestic violence. Women who were housewives/mothers with no income — but NOT escaping violence — have nowhere to go.

---

### The Solution

**10-20 women + children** at a time live on our cabin-style campus:

- **Single cabins** — 4 units for individual women
- **Family cabins** — 6 units for mothers with children
- **Common building** — Kitchen, dining, community space
- **Farm** — Therapeutic work, skill-building

**6-24 month program** with case management, job training, financial literacy, homeschool co-op, and support.

---

### Impact Goal

**80%+ of graduates** move to permanent housing with employment or education path.
