# We Elevate

*A transitional living center for women rebuilding their lives — built together*

---

## The Problem

There's a gap in the support system for women facing financial crisis after years of marriage. Traditional shelters serve those in immediate physical danger — and that's critical work. But what about the woman who's not in danger, but has no income, no job skills, and no way to support herself after a divorce or losing her husband?

She falls through the cracks.

## The Solution

**We Elevate** is a temporary residence and rehabilitation center designed specifically for women in this situation — women who were married for several years, often with children, who now find themselves stranded without resources or a path forward.

It's not a shelter. It's a launchpad.

## How It Works

**Housing** — Safe, temporary homes for women and their children

**Farm & Gardens** — Physical work that grounds, heals, and feeds the community

**Workshops** — Job skills, financial literacy, life skills — open to the community

**Homeschool Co-op** — Moms lead and teach, building confidence

**Healing** — Emotional and spiritual support for grief, loss, transition

**Social Enterprise** — Farmers market, resident businesses, program merch — residents build real skills and small businesses

**Community Engagement & Outreach** — The gardens and workshops aren't just for residents. They're open to the wider community — creating connections, sharing knowledge, and building a network of support that extends beyond our walls.

**Personal Growth** — This is at the heart of everything. We help women rediscover themselves, their strengths, and their potential.

**Mental Health & Wellbeing** — We're not licensed professionals. What we offer is just as powerful: self-help strategies woven into daily life. Art therapy. Group meditation. Shared meals and community gatherings where women can connect, be heard, and heal together. We create the space for wellness — and we partner with licensed professionals when additional support is needed.

## Phase 2: Tiny House Village

Here's what happens when they graduate. We've reserved 10 acres for a tiny house village — graduates can rent or buy an affordable home and stay in the community they've built.

**Volunteer build events** — Like Habitat for Humanity, we organize weekend blitz builds. Hardware store flyers, community volunteers, near-zero labor costs.

**Alumni leadership** — Our first graduates become peer mentors for the next group. They know the program because they lived it. Eventually, they can run the whole place.

---

## Philosophy

Community over isolation. Private spaces without isolating people. Everyone contributes, everyone grows. Not black-and-white criteria. Limited spots to ensure quality over quantity.

---

## Why Cameron, Texas

Affordable land, supportive community, and Brittany & Heather will live on-site together. The small town (population ~5,300) means real connection, not institutional anonymity.

---

## The Model

This isn't a handout — it's a hand up. Women come with their children, rebuild skills and confidence, and transition to independent lives.
