# We Elevate — Speech

---

When people think about women in crisis, they think about shelters. And that's important — those shelters save lives. But there's a whole group of women who don't fit that picture. Women who've been married for years. Women who stayed home. Women who raised kids. And then, suddenly, they're alone. No income. No job skills. No way to support themselves or their children.

They're not in danger. But they're stuck. And there's nowhere for them to go.

That's where **We Elevate** comes in.

We Elevate is a temporary residence and rehabilitation center for women rebuilding their lives after divorce or widowhood. It's not a shelter — it's a launchpad.

Here's what makes us different. We don't just provide a place to stay. We provide a path forward.

Our women live in simple, private cabins with their children. They work on our farm and in our gardens — physical work that's grounding, therapeutic, and feeds the community. They learn real skills through workshops we open to the community — job training, financial literacy, life skills. Mothers lead our homeschool co-op, giving them purpose and teaching opportunities.

We focus on personal growth, on healing — emotional and spiritual — and on mental wellbeing. Now, I'm not a licensed therapist. But what we offer is powerful: art therapy, group meditation, shared meals, community gatherings. A space where women can be heard, connect, and heal together. And when someone needs more, we connect them with professionals.

The farm itself is part of the healing. So is the farmers market where residents can build real businesses. So is everything we do.

**But here's what happens after they graduate.** We've reserved 10 acres for a tiny house village — graduates can rent or buy an affordable home and stay in the community they've built. We'll organize volunteer build events, Habitat-style, where residents can even help build their own. And our first graduates become peer mentors for the next group — they know the program because they lived it.

I dreamed this up. But I won't do it alone. My sister Heather will be right there with me. We'll both live on-site. This is our shared mission.

We're building this in Cameron, Texas — a small town where land is affordable and community is real. Not some big institution. A place that feels like home.

This isn't a handout. It's a hand up. Women come, they rebuild their confidence and their skills, and they transition to independent lives.

Now, here's the honest part. I don't have money to fund this. Neither does Heather. Everything — 100% — has to be raised through grants, crowdfunding, donations, and fundraisers.

Our startup cost for a minimum viable facility is between 253 and 366 thousand dollars. That's six cabins, a common building, site work, permits. We still need land — through lease or donation.

We're not asking for easy. We're asking for real.

This is about women who fell through the cracks. Women who deserve a second chance. Women who just need someone to believe in them long enough to believe in themselves.

That's what We Elevate is for.
