## Research: The Gatehouse (Similar Model in DFW)

I checked their website — they don't publicly list specific corporate sponsors, but here's what they offer corporate partners:

**Corporate Partnership Types (The Gatehouse Model):**
- Community Partner (logo on website, newsletter mentions, volunteer days)
- Team volunteer opportunities
- Grant program participation
- Donation drives
- Event sponsorships

**Funding sources they mention:**
- Rose Family Foundation (initial grant)
- Private donors
- Foundations
- Corporations
- Churches

**Key insight:** They started with ONE foundation grant, then built from there.

*For We Elevate — we can model our own corporate partnership program after this structure.*
