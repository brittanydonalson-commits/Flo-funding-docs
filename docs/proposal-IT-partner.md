### Introduction

I am writing to explore a potential partnership opportunity in government contracting. I am in the process of establishing a business entity that will serve as a prime contractor for federal, state, and local government contracts, with a focus on IT services and support.

The purpose of this proposal is to outline how we could work together — I would handle business development, contract acquisition, and client relationships, while your team would perform the technical work as a subcontractor.

---

### The Opportunity

The federal government spends billions of dollars annually on IT services, including:
- Help desk and technical support
- Network administration
- System maintenance
- Cybersecurity support

Small businesses can access many of these opportunities through set-aside programs (WOSB, SDVOSB, 8(a), SDVOB), which reduce competition and reserved opportunities for eligible businesses.

---

### Proposed Partnership Structure

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Prime Contractor (Me)** | Acquire contracts, manage client relationships, handle proposals and bidding, maintain SAM.gov registration, pursue certifications |
| **Subcontractor (You)** | Perform the technical/IT work as outlined in each contract, provide quality delivery, support past performance documentation |

**Revenue Model:** You would receive payment for work performed under each contract. The margin between the contract value and your fees would support my business operations and acquisition efforts.
