# We Elevate
## Transitional Living Center for Women in Transition

---

### The Vision

I know what it's like to give your whole life to something and have it fall apart. WeElevate is for women who've done exactly that — and deserve a chance to rebuild.

We provide:
- Safe housing (cabins with shared community building)
- Life skills & job training workshops
- Financial literacy education
- Homeschool co-op led by residents
- Spiritual & emotional support
- Path to self-sufficiency
- **Phase 2: Tiny House Village** for graduates — affordable rent or buy options
- **Alumni leadership** — graduates become peer mentors for next residents

---

### The Need

**This population is underserved by existing resources:**

- **41%** of first marriages end in divorce
- **73%** income reduction for women after divorce (vs. 41% for men)
- **13 million widows** in the US; many never worked outside the home
- Traditional shelters focus on domestic violence — not this gap

**In Texas:**
- **6,339** families with children experiencing homelessness
- **39% increase** in family homelessness (2023-2024)
