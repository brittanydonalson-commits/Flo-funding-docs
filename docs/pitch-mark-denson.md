## We Elevate

**A transitional living community for women and their children**

Here's the situation I'm addressing: There are women who have been housewives, stay-at-home moms, married for years — and then find themselves in transition through divorce or widowhood with no income, no job skills, and no support system. Traditional shelters are designed for physical safety, which is critical — but they don't address this specific gap: women who are financially stranded with children and no way out.

**What we're building:** A safe, temporary residence where women can rebuild — through housing, community, workshops (job skills, financial literacy, life skills), a working farm/garden for healing and purpose, and pathways to independence. We'll also host existing local homeschool co-ops for residents who want to homeschool their children.

**The model:** I'm partnering with my sister Heather and my stepdaughter Shelby on this. We'll live on-site together. It's designed to be small-scale and community-driven.

**The need:** We're raising for the initial facility (5 duplex cabins housing 10 families, plus common areas). The goal is to keep it lean — volunteer builds, community involvement, partnerships with local businesses.

**Why I'm reaching out:** I know you've got heart for giving back and connections in the community. If this resonates with you — whether through investment, intro to others who might want to be part of this, or just a conversation — I'd love to talk it through.

No pressure at all. I just believe in this vision and wanted to share it with you.
