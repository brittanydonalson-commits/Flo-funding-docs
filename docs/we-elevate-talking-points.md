# We Elevate — Talking Points

---

## The Problem

- Traditional shelters serve women in immediate physical danger
- There's a gap: women who aren't in danger but have no income, no job skills, no way forward
- Women married for years, stayed home, raised kids — then left with nothing
- These women fall through the cracks

---

## The Solution

- We Elevate: temporary residence + rehabilitation center
- Not a shelter — a launchpad

---

## What We Offer

1. **Housing** — Safe, private cabins for women and their children
2. **Farm & Gardens** — Therapeutic work that feeds the community
3. **Workshops** — Job skills, financial literacy, life skills — open to community
4. **Homeschool Co-op** — Moms teach, building confidence and purpose
5. **Emotional healing** — Support for grief, loss, divorce, widowhood
6. **Personal growth** — Rediscover strengths and potential
7. **Mental health & wellbeing** — Self-help strategies: art therapy, meditation, shared meals, community gatherings; partner with professionals when needed
8. **Social enterprise** — Farmers market, resident businesses, program merch
9. **Community outreach** — Gardens and workshops open to wider community

---

## The Team

- Brittany (founder) + Heather (sister) — both live on-site

---

## Location

- Cameron, Texas (pop. 5,300)
- Affordable land, real community connection
- Small-town feel, not institutional

---

## Philosophy

- Community over isolation
- Privacy without isolating people
- Not black-and-white criteria
- Limited spots = quality over quantity

---

## The Ask

- Brittany and Heather have zero personal funding
- 100% must be raised: grants, crowdfunding, donations, fundraisers
- Startup cost: $253K–$366K (6-unit minimum viable)
- Land not included — needs lease or donation

---

## The Bottom Line

- "This isn't a handout — it's a hand up"
- Women rebuild confidence and skills, then transition to independent lives
- About women who deserve a second chance
