# We Elevate — Vision

*A transitional living community for women and their children*

---

## The Problem

Every year, thousands of women find themselves in transition through divorce or widowhood with:
- No independent income
- No job skills or recent work experience
- Children to provide for
- No support system

Traditional shelters are designed for physical safety — and that's critical. But they don't address this specific gap: women who are financially stranded, not physically unsafe.

**They need a bridge to independence, not just a place to stay.**

---

## Our Solution

**We Elevate** is a transitional living community where women and their children can rebuild their lives with dignity.

### What We Offer
- **Safe, temporary housing** — A home while they get back on their feet
- **Community** — Peer support from women facing similar challenges
- **Workshops** — Job skills, financial literacy, life skills, confidence-building
- **Working farm/garden** — Therapeutic purpose, physical work, connection to earth
- **Pathways to independence** — Each resident works toward her own next step

### Who We Serve
- Women who were housewives or stay-at-home moms
- Women going through divorce or who have lost a spouse
- Women with children who have no income path
- Women who want to rebuild, not just survive

## Why It Matters

- **~40% of marriages** will end in divorce
- **28% of single mothers** live in poverty
- Children of divorce face **9-13% lower income** in adulthood
- **24 million children** in the U.S. live in single-parent families
- Family homelessness hit **record highs** in 2024

This population exists in a gap — too "stable" for emergency shelter, too financially stranded to make it alone. **We fill that gap.**

## The Philosophy

1. **Housing as a foundation** — Safe, temporary residence
2. **Farm as healing** — Physical work, grounding, purpose
3. **Workshops for growth** — Skills for independence
4. **Community over isolation** — Connection, not codependence
5. **Spiritual & emotional wellness** — Inner work, not religious doctrine
6. **Not black-and-white criteria** — We help who we can, when we can
7. **Residents are family** — Not clients, not case numbers
