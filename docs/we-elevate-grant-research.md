## Priority Grants (Act Fast)

### 1. TSAHC Texas Foundations Fund
**Deadline: August 1, 2026** ⚠️ Coming up soon!
- **Amount:** Up to $1.2M (but typical much smaller)
- **Focus:** Very low-income households, supportive housing services
- **Eligible:** 501(c)(3) nonprofits
- **Apply:** https://tsahc.smapply.io/prog/texas_foundations_fund_2026/
- **Notes:** Housing counseling, supportive services for homeless/very low-income

---

### 2. HUD Emergency Solutions Grants (ESG)
- **Amount:** ~$9.3M/year statewide (Texas)
- **Focus:** Homeless prevention, rapid re-housing, transitional housing
- **Admin:** Texas Department of Housing & Community Affairs (TDHCA)
- **Eligible:** 501(c)(3) nonprofits, local governments
- **Apply:** Through TDHCA (www.tdhca.texas.gov/esg-funding)
- **Notes:** Can fund operational costs, case management, housing

---

### 3. HUD Continuum of Care (CoC) — Transitional Housing
- **Amount:** Varies (competitive)
- **Focus:** Transitional housing for homeless populations
- **Apply:** Annual NOFO from HUD (hud.gov)
- **Notes:** Requires 501(c)(3) + demonstrated need

---

## USDA / Farm / Rural Programs

### 4. USDA Rural Development Community Facilities
- **Amount:** Up to 75% of project cost (grant + loan combo)
- **Focus:** Rural community facilities (can include transitional housing)
- **Location:** Must be rural (Cameron qualifies — pop. 5,306)
- **Apply:** Local USDA RD office
- **Notes:** Great for facility construction in rural areas

### 5. USDA Community Food Projects Grant (CFPCGP)
- **Amount:** Up to $400,000 (5-year grant)
- **Focus:** Community food systems, gardens, farms, food security
- **Eligible:** 501(c)(3) nonprofits
- **Apply:** grants.gov
- **Notes:** Perfect for the farm/garden component

### 6. USDA Farm Service Agency (FSA) Programs
- **Focus:** Beginning farmers, small farms, agricultural education
- **Notes:** May qualify for equipment, supplies, training programs
