**Project Name:** Wanderlust & Co. Transitional Living Center
**Organization Type:** 501(c)(3) Nonprofit (to be formed)
**Location:** Rural Texas (target community: TBD)
**Target Population:** Women in transition (divorcees/widows with no independent income) and their children

---

## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Wanderlust & Co. is a transitional living center designed to address a critical gap in housing assistance: **women who have no independent income after divorce or widowhood** — distinct from domestic violence populations served by existing shelters.

Our facility provides:
- Safe, temporary housing for women and their children
- Life skills, job training, and financial literacy workshops
- Homeschool co-op opportunities led by residents
- Spiritual and emotional support
- Path to self-sufficiency

**Funding Request:** $60,000 - $100,000 for facility construction (cabins + common building)

---

## II. THE NEED (Citations)

### National Context
- **41%** of first marriages end in divorce (APA 2025)
- Divorce reduces women's household income by **73%** (men: 41%) — WorldMetrics
- **40%** of divorcing individuals experience credit score drops
- **13 million widows** in the US; avg. Social Security benefit: **$1,774/month**
- **259,473** family members experience homelessness (HUD 2024) — 39% increase YoY

### Texas-Specific Data
- Population: **30.5 million** (2nd largest US state)
- **6,339** people in families with children homeless (2024)
- Family homelessness **increased 39%** from 2023-2024
- **69%** of Texas divorces filed by women
- Median marriage length before divorce: **17.5 years**
- Divorce filings **increased 3.7%** in 2024 vs 2023

### The Gap We Fill
- Existing transitional housing focuses on **domestic violence survivors**
- **No resources exist** for financially stranded divorcees/widows who are NOT escaping violence but have no income, no job skills, no path to self-sufficiency
- This population falls through the cracks of both DV shelters and general homeless services

## III. PROGRAM DESIGN

### Housing Components
- **4 single cabins** (12'x12') — Individual women
- **6 family cabins with loft** (12'x16') — Mothers with children
- **Common building** (30'x40' enclosed metal barn) — Kitchen, dining, community space
- **Shared bathhouse & laundry** facilities

### Program Services
1. **Housing** — Safe, temporary residence (enclosed, air-conditioned for Texas climate)
2. **Farm/Outdoor Work** — Therapeutic, grounding, skill-building
3. **Workshops** — Job skills, financial literacy, life skills
4. **Homeschool Co-op** — Led by moms (builds purpose and teaching opportunity)
5. **Emotional/Spiritual Healing** — Processing grief, loss, divorce, widowhood
6. **Confidence Building** — Rebuilding self-worth through achievement
7. **Social Enterprise** — Farmers market, resident businesses

### Target Demographics
- Women who were housewives/stay-at-home mothers
- Going through divorce OR widowed
- No independent income, no employment history
- May have children
- No other support system
