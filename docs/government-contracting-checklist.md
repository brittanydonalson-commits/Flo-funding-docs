# Government Contracting Setup Checklist

## Overview

**The Model:**
- You (Brittany) = Prime contractor → win contracts, handle business side, keep margin
- Your IT partner = Subcontractor → performs the actual IT work

**What She Brings:**
- Years of IT experience
- Management experience  
- Business ownership experience
- Labor force

**What You Bring:**
- Business ownership (you hold the contract)
- Certifications (WOSB, etc. if eligible)
- Sales/bidding capability

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Do First)

### 1. Form LLC (Texas)

**Where to file:** [sos.state.tx.us/corp](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp)
- Use **SOSDirect** for online filing
- Or: [direct.sos.state.tx.us/lef](https://direct.sos.state.tx.us/lef/index.aspx)

**Cost:** ~$300 (filing fee $300 + expedite fee optional)

**Turnaround:** 3-5 business days standard, 1-2 days with expedite

### 2. Get EIN

**Apply free at:** [irs.gov/ein](https://irs.gov/ein)
- Takes ~5 minutes
- Apply after LLC (or concurrently)

### 3. SAM.gov Registration

**Go to:** [sam.gov/entity-registration](https://sam.gov/entity-registration)

Complete full entity registration (not just UEI):
- [ ] Create login (or use Login.gov)
- [ ] Enter LLC legal name
- [ ] Enter EIN from IRS
- [ ] Provide business address
- [ ] Add NAICS codes (see below)

### 4. NAICS Codes to Add

| Code | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **541513** | Computer Facilities Management Services (tech support, help desk, IT ops) |
| 541511 | Custom Computer Programming Services |
| 541512 | Computer Systems Design Services |
| 541519 | Other Computer Related Services |

**Priority:** Focus on **541513** for tech support contracts.

## Phase 2: Position for Success

### 5. Certifications (Optional but Recommended)

| Certification | Who Qualifies | Why It Helps |
|---------------|---------------|--------------|
| **WOSB** | Women-owned | Access to set-aside contracts, less competition |
| **SDVOB** | Service-disabled veteran-owned | Set-aside access |
| **8(a)** | Socially/economically disadvantaged | Business development + set-asides |

**How to apply:** Via SAM.gov certifications section after registration

### 6. Build Your Capability Statement

A 1-page document that includes:
- Company name, address, contact info
- What you do (focus on tech support/services)
- Your NAICS codes
- Past performance (from your IT partner's commercial work)
- Certifications
- Point of contact

### 7. Past Performance

Even without government experience, you can use:
- Commercial contracts (your IT partner's corporate clients)
- Past performance questionnaires from non-gov clients
- Demonstrated capability through team resumes/CVs

---

## Phase 3: Find & Win Opportunities

### 8. Search for Contracts

**Primary source:** [sam.gov/contracting](https://sam.gov/contracting)

**Search filters to use:**
- NAICS code: 541513
- Set-aside: "Small Business"
- Status: "Active"

**Also check:**
- [gsa.gov](https://www.gsa.gov) — GSA Schedule contracts
- State/local contracts (easier to break in)

### 9. Start Bidding Strategy

**Begin with:**
- Smaller contracts ($250K or less)
- Set-aside competitions (fewer bidders)
- Local/regional opportunities

**Consider:**
- Subcontracting under an experienced prime first
- Building relationships with contracting officers
