## Divorce in America

- **~40% of first marriages** will end in divorce (Institute for Family Studies, 2024)
- **14.2 divorced women per 1,000 married women** in 2024 (refined divorce rate, BGSU)
- Divorce rates hit a 50-year low in 2024, but raw numbers remain significant

---

## Single-Parent Households

- **Nearly 24 million children** in the U.S. live in a single-parent family
- **~80% of single-parent households** are headed by women
- Single mothers are **2x more likely** to experience poverty than single fathers (FRED, 2024)
- **28% poverty rate** for single mothers (2022) vs. 15% for single fathers

---

## Impact on Children

According to recent Census Bureau and NBER research:

- **Income reduction:** Children whose parents divorce in early childhood see a **9-13% reduction in income** by their mid-to-late 20s
- **Teen birth rates:** Increase by **63%** following parental divorce
- **Early mortality risk:** Increased by **35-55%** at the time of divorce, remaining elevated into adulthood
- **Educational attainment:** Lower levels for children of divorce, particularly boys
- **Long-term earnings:** Persistent negative effects on adult earnings

*Source: Census Bureau Working Paper (2025), NBER (2025)*

## Homelessness

- **745,652 people** homeless on a single night in January 2025 (HUD AHAR 2025)
- **Families with children** experienced the **largest year-over-year increase** in homelessness of any group (2023-2024)
- Family homelessness reached **highest recorded numbers** in 2024

---

## The Gap We're Addressing

Traditional shelters serve:
- Physical safety (domestic violence)
- Immediate crisis

**What's missing:**
- Transitional housing for women who were housewives/stay-at-home moms with no income path
- Women who lost a spouse and have no independent income
- A pathway to self-sufficiency, not just a place to stay

This population — financially stranded, no job skills, no support system — falls through every crack in the system. They don't need emergency shelter. They need a **bridge to independence**.
