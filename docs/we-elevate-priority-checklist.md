# We Elevate — Priority Checklist

## Phase 1: Land Acquisition (NOW - Without land, nothing else matters)

**Target: 50–100 acres** (better to have more than not enough)

- [ ] **Visit Cameron, Texas** — scout locations, feel the area
- [ ] **Talk to churches** — present outreach one-pager, explore land donation/lease
- [ ] **Talk to county/local officials** — zoning, available properties, incentive programs
- [ ] **Search land listings** — Texas land for sale/lease in Milam County area (50+ acres)
- [ ] **Network locally** — let people know what you're looking for

## Phase 2: Foundation (While finding land)

- [ ] **File Texas LLC** — formalize the organization
- [ ] **Get EIN** — for the organization
- [ ] **Apply for 501(c)(3)** — nonprofit status (or find fiscal sponsor)
- [ ] **Create detailed budget** — refine cost estimates, operational costs

## Phase 3: Funding (After land is secured)

- [ ] **Research grant options** — HUD CoC, ESG, Texas housing programs, private foundations
- [ ] **Build grant pipeline** — identify top grants to apply for
- [ ] **Start crowdfunding** — platform selection, campaign planning

## Phase 4: Program Design

- [ ] **Research similar programs** — find what's working elsewhere
- [ ] **Define qualifying criteria** — who gets in, how selected
- [ ] **Define length of stay** — 3 months? 6 months? 1 year?
- [ ] **Map program offerings** — job training, financial literacy, counseling, etc.
- [ ] **Design facility layout** — finalize cabin/facility plans

## Phase 5: Build & Launch

- [ ] **Build/renovate** — or purchase property
- [ ] **Hire/recruit staff** — or use volunteers initially
- [ ] **Open for residents**
- [ ] **Ongoing fundraising**
