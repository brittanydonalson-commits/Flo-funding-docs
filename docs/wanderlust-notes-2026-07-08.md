# Wanderlust & Co. - Project Notes
## Date: 2026-07-08

---

## Vision

Transitional living center for women in transition (divorcees/widows with no independent income) and their children.

**Target Population:**
- Women married for several years
- Going through divorce OR widowed
- Housewives / stay-at-home moms with no independent income
- Likely have children
- No support system, no way to support themselves

**Location:** Texas (rural)

---

## Facility Design

### Housing Layout (Camp/Rehab Style)
- **Single cabins** — Basic, minimalist, efficient, aesthetically pleasing
- **Family cabins with loft** — For mothers with children
- **Community building** — Shared kitchen, dining area
- **Shared facilities** — His/her bathroom & shower blocks, laundry center

**Design Philosophy:** Privacy without isolation. Encourages community, prevents isolating.

### Funding Sources to Research

**Grants:**
- **HUD ESG** — Texas admin through TDHCA, ~$9.3M/year statewide, nonprofits eligible, focuses on homelessness prevention, rapid re-housing, emergency shelter
- **USDA Rural Development Community Facilities** — EXACT MATCH for this project! Provides loans + grants for "transitional housing" in rural areas (≤20k pop). Grants up to 75% for small low-income communities. 
- **Texas State Affordable Housing Corporation (TSAHC) Texas Foundations Fund** — $1.2M+ annually for nonprofits, supports housing programs for very low-income households. Deadline: August 1, 2026 (rolling until funding depleted)
- **TDHCA NOFAs** — Texas Dept of Housing & Community Affairs posts funding opportunities throughout year at tdhca.texas.gov
- **Instrumentl** — 400+ active Texas grants for nonprofits, $137.8M total, $61.3K median. Categories: Affordable Housing, Community Development, Human Services, etc.
- Texas Department of Agriculture grants
- Private foundations (women's shelters, family services)

**Land Options:**
- **Land trusts** (TX Land Trust Council, Farmers Land Trust) — land conservation orgs that may partner
- **Church/religious organization land donations** — Many churches have rural land and may donate for ministry
- **County surplus land** — Some counties sell/dispose of unused land cheaply
- **Private landowner partnerships** (lease cheap) — Some landowners lease for "community benefit"
- **USDA farmland programs** — May have land access programs

**Note:** Land donations to 501(c)(3) nonprofits are tax-deductible for the donor, which helps attract donors.

**Key Insight:** Most transitional housing = domestic violence focus. Brittany's unique gap = women divorced/widowed who are NOT escaping violence but have no income/skills to support themselves. This is an underserved population.

### "The Need" — Statistics for Grant Applications

#### Divorce Statistics
- **41% of first marriages** end in divorce (APA 2025 estimate)
- **673,989 divorces** in 2022 (45 states reporting)
- **2,065,905 marriages** in 2022 — far more divorces happening
- Median age at first divorce: **30.5 men / 28.6 women**
- Peak divorce rate: **25-29 age group** at 24.9 per 1,000 women
- **Average divorce costs $15,000** (up to $30,000 for contested)
- Divorce reduces household income by **73% for women, 41% for men**
- **40% experience credit score drop** from shared debt division
- **30% of divorces** involve child custody disputes

#### "Gray Divorce" (50+)
- Divorce rate **doubled since 1990s** for 50+
- **Tripled for 65+**
- Women at high financial risk — often out of workforce for years

#### Widowhood
- **13 million widows** in the US
- Average Social Security benefit for widows: **$1,774/month** (2023)
- Women typically outlive husbands by 5-7 years
- Many widows never worked outside home

#### Why This Matters for Our Cause
- Women who were housewives/mothers have **no employment history, no skills, no income path**
- After divorce or widowhood, they face **immediate financial crisis**
- Traditional shelters focus on **domestic violence** — not this population
- **No resources exist** for financially stranded stay-at-home moms
- This is a **documented gap** in the housing assistance ecosystem

#### Texas-Specific Data

**Population:** 30.5 million (2nd largest US state)

**Divorce Statistics:**
- Crude divorce rate: **2.1-2.7 per 1,000** (below national average, among lowest in US)
- **69% of divorces** initiated by women
- Median marriage length before divorce: **17.5-17.6 years**
- Gray divorce (50+): **increasing** — long-term marriages ending
- Divorce filings **increased 3.7%** in 2024 vs 2023
- Average divorce cost: **$15,000** (contested)

**Homelessness in Texas:**
- **6,339 people in families with children** experiencing homelessness (2024)
- Family homelessness **increased 39%** from 2023 to 2024
- Overall homelessness **up 18%** year-over-year
- **102,197** Texans entered homelessness in 2024
- Only **16,049** permanently housed — huge gap

**Why Texas Makes Sense:**
- Large population = larger need
- Below-average divorce rate = but still ~80,000+ divorces/year given population
- Growing family homelessness crisis
- Strong USDA Rural Development funding for rural areas
- Texas is a **community property state** — women often leave with half assets but no income to support themselves

### Benefits for Children

**The Crisis:**
- **259,473** family members experience homelessness (HUD 2024) — **1/3 of all homeless**
- Family homelessness **rose 39%** from prior year — largest increase of any group
- **30% of divorces** involve child custody disputes
- Children in homeless families face trauma, school disruption, health issues

**What Stable Housing Provides:**
- **Educational continuity** — Kids can stay in same school
- **Emotional stability** — Safe, predictable environment
- **Health improvements** — Access to proper nutrition, healthcare
- **Future outcomes** — Stable childhood → better adult outcomes
- **Break the cycle** — Prevention rather than crisis intervention

**Research shows** transitional housing programs improve:
- Housing stability
- Mental health
- Employment/education outcomes
- Independent living skills

**The ROI Argument:**
- Every child who avoids homelessness = **reduced foster care, emergency services, juvenile justice costs**
- Investing in families now = **fewer costs to society later**
- Education + stability = **path to self-sufficiency**

## Church Land Donation — Action Plan

### Why Churches Are Ideal Partners
- Land donations to 501(c)(3) nonprofits are **tax-deductible** for the donor
- Many rural churches have land they're not using
- Faith-based mission aligns with helping women in crisis
- Ministry opportunity — "doing God's work"

### How to Approach Churches

**Step 1: Identify Churches in Cameron/Milam County**
- Drive around Cameron — note churches you see
- Ask locals: "Which churches are active here?"
- Check with county courthouse for any church-owned land

**Step 2: Prepare Your Pitch**
- Brief explanation of the mission (help women in transition)
- What you're looking for (land donation or long-term lease)
- Tax benefits for the church
- How it serves the community

**Step 3: What to Ask**
- "Do you have any land that isn't being used?"
- "Would your church consider donating land to a nonprofit helping women in crisis?"
- "Can we meet to discuss a potential partnership?"

**Step 4: Bring Materials**
- One-page summary of the project
- Your background (Brittany)
- Vision/mission statement
- Contact info
