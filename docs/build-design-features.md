# We Elevate — Build & Design Features

*Comprehensive vision for the campus*

---

## Housing
- **Housing/roommate style economy tiny cabins** — Duplex style, flexible configuration

## Gardens & Agriculture
- **Fruit/Vegetable Gardens** — Fresh produce for residents
- **Small orchard for essentials** — Fruit trees
- **Botanical/Herb Garden** — Herbs for cooking, wellness
- **Children's garden** — Teaching opportunities, workshops, hosting field trips
- **Composting station** — Sustainable waste cycle

## Livestock
- **Chicken coup** — Eggs
- **Beehives** — Honey, pollination
- **Barn and pasture areas** for:
  - Miniature dairy cows
  - Goats
  - Sheep or lamb

## Water Systems
- **Water/rain catch system** — Rainwater collection
- **Down line and clear natural filtration tanks** — Custom built as educational tools AND visually appealing

## Outdoor Cooking & Gathering
- **Covered outdoor kitchen area** — Covered outdoor cooking space
- **Brick and stone oven** — Wood-fired
- **Fire pit 1** — For oversized cast iron pot (cob cooking)
- **Fire pit 2** — For BBQ grills and picnic tables

## Indoor Community
- **Indoor community kitchen** — Industrial style setup

## Resident Facilities
- **Shower/Bath house** — For residents only
- **Laundry facilities** — For residents

## Community Engagement
- **Farmers Market** — Vendor booths for residents or local businesses/artisans
- **Education Center** — Workshops, homeschool co-op hosting, provider presentations

## Wellness
- **Wellness Center** — For residents AND possibly paying customers
  - Reflection pond / meditation zone
  - Yoga studio
  - Hot and cold therapeutic soaking tubs

## Future Growth
- **Designated land for future tiny house community** — Phase 2 graduate housing
- **Designated areas for seasonal activities:**
  - Pumpkin patch
  - Christmas tree farm
  - Watermelon patch
